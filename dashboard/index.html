How implied value is computed

implied = TVL × 0.30 × growth × maturity × breadth × category

CategoryMultipleWhy

Three factors do the work: size (TVL), growth (the last seven days) and maturity (how long the protocol has run with no token). Breadth and category adjust the result by at most +20% and ±15%. Every row below can be checked: open a protocol and the detail view shows each factor and the product, and the page recomputes the number from the snapshot figures. No protocol is ranked by its name.

Data: DefiLlama, snapshot 14 Nov 2026. Model radar-2. Implied value is a ranking device, not a price forecast and not financial advice. A protocol can launch, delay, or never launch; farming costs gas and time and may return nothing.
